Mechanics

Master weapons that suit your soul: swift blades, crushing hammers, or cleaving axes.

Rely on acrobatic skills: wall jump, dodge, and wall run.

Change the world with your will: stop time, reverse events, or burn obstacles in your path. The main thing is to reach the Truth...

World

The worlds of idealism and materialism have reunited: any thought can become reality if you have enough willpower...

You wake up with amnesia. You don't know who or what you are, but one thing is certain — you must reach the place or object that has been calling to you since awakening: the point of Superposition... Truth... Where the beginning and the end meet.

You will face insurmountable difficulties along the way. It seems there is no way out — but with enough willpower, anything is possible.

Developer's Log

The Project Solus is the first video game I showed to the public, albeit in the form of a social media video. Before that, I tried many times to bring to life a video game I would like to play, but each time I hit the wall of not knowing the basics of virtual worlds — programming and game design. And now, 4 years later, I have completed my bachelor's degree in IT, read many books and articles on game design, and I am confident that I am now ready to move forward not only on enthusiasm, but also on the skills and knowledge I have acquired.
